# Documents required for a personal loan

This page is a fictional Harbourline Bank POC example.

## Salaried customers

- Identity and address are already held on the verified profile.
- Latest salary slip.
- A bank statement is not needed in this POC when the salary slip states net monthly income.

## Self-employed customers

- Latest income tax return.
- Identity and address are already held on the verified profile.

## Unreadable files

Photographs are not read by this POC. Upload a text or PDF salary slip.
