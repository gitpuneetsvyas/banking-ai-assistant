# EMI calculation

This page is a fictional Harbourline Bank POC example.

An EMI is an equated monthly instalment. EMI is calculated as P times r times (1+r) to the power n, divided by ((1+r) to the power n minus 1). P is the principal, r is the monthly interest rate, and n is the number of months. This POC illustrates an EMI with the fictional personal-loan rate of 12.5% per annum only. An illustration is not a sanction.
