# Personal loan eligibility requirements

This page is a fictional Harbourline Bank POC example. It is not a real credit policy or an offer.

## Criteria

A customer qualifies for a personal loan when all of the following are true:

- Age is 21 to 60 years on the application date.
- Employment is salaried or self-employed.
- Monthly income is at least INR 25000.
- The amount is INR 50000 to INR 1500000.
- Tenure is 12 to 60 months.
- The amount is not more than 24 times monthly income.
- KYC on the customer profile is verified.

## Illustrative rate

The illustrative POC interest rate is 12.5% per annum on a reducing balance. This rate is fictional and is not a sanction.

## Fees that apply

Fees that apply to a personal loan are a processing fee of 2% of the sanctioned amount, capped at INR 10000. No other personal-loan fee is published here.
