# Home loan eligibility requirements

This page is a fictional Harbourline Bank POC example. It is not a real offer.

Home loan eligibility requirements in this fictional POC are an amount of INR 500000 to INR 10000000, a tenure of 60 to 240 months, and an illustrative rate of 8.5% per annum. Property papers are expected. Fees that apply to a home loan are a processing fee of 1% of the sanctioned amount, capped at INR 25000.
